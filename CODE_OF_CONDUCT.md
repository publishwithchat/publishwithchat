# Code of conduct

We want this to be a friendly, useful place for everyone, whatever their experience.

- Be respectful and patient. Many people here are new to chatbots and websites.
- Stay on topic and keep it constructive.
- No harassment, discrimination, abuse or spam.
- Do not share other people's private information.

We may edit or remove comments and close issues that do not follow this, and block people who repeatedly ignore it. To report a problem, email support@publishwith.chat.
