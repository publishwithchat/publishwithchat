# Publish with chat brand pack

Open `brand-guidelines.html` first. It explains the logo, colours, type, tone and every file.

- `logo/` mark, horizontal and stacked lockups, in colour, reversed (for dark backgrounds), and one-colour ink and white. SVG and PNG.
- `icons/` favicons, iPhone and Android icons, a maskable icon, and the 512 pixel directory icon.
- `social/` link-preview image (1200 x 630), profile pictures, square posts.
- `banners/` X, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube and email headers, each in dark and light.

All text inside the images is converted to outlines, so they look the same everywhere with no fonts to install.

## Using the brand

You are welcome to use the name and logo to say that you use, review, link to or write about Publish with chat, for example in a blog post, a directory listing, a tutorial or a "works with" list.

Please:
- Use the files as supplied. Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, crop or redraw them.
- Leave clear space around the logo, roughly the height of the green dot on every side.
- Do not use them in a way that suggests we made, endorse or are affiliated with your product, or in the name of your own product, domain or social account.

The logos, icons and name remain the property of Conduit Digital. Questions or something else you need: support@publishwith.chat.
