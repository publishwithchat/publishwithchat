# Troubleshooting

## I cannot find where to add a connector

Some chatbots only allow custom connectors on certain plans, or hide them under another name (Connectors, Apps, Plugins, Developer mode). See [connect](connect.md) for the usual path in each. If your plan does not allow it, that is the chatbot's rule.

## Gemini says it cannot connect

Turn on Gemini Apps Activity in your Google account. Without it the connection fails. Then add the app again at gemini.google.com/devmode.

## Gemini stopped halfway

Gemini pauses after a few steps in one reply. Tell it to carry on.

## ChatGPT keeps asking me to approve

That is normal. ChatGPT asks before each action. Approve each step.

## The chatbot made a site but I cannot change it later

Changing a site you already made needs you to be signed in. In Claude, turn on sign in when needed. In ChatGPT, pick OAuth when you add the connector. You will be asked to sign in the first time.

## My preview link stopped working

Preview links last 48 hours. The files are kept for 14 days, so you can still claim the site and put it online. Ask your chatbot to publish it again, or use the link in the email we sent if you gave your address.

## The chatbot says my files have a problem

Publish with chat checks every upload. It lists errors (which block the preview) and warnings (worth fixing). Ask the chatbot to fix what it reported and send the site again.

## My images look wrong

Original images are stored exactly as you attach them, with no resizing. If an image does not appear, make sure the chatbot saved it at the same file name the page uses.

## Still stuck

Open an [issue](https://github.com/publishwithchat/publishwithchat/issues/new/choose) or email support@publishwith.chat. Tell us which chatbot you used and what it said.
